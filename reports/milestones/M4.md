TMP/TEMP set to results as previously documented. 28 tests passed.
Records have window ID, ending round ID, complete-window arrival, finish and
queue delay. Windows require rounds of context plus terminal memory measurement;
no early correction is implied. The model assumes terminal measurement adds no
extra time beyond the supplied window period. No real quantum interface is modeled.

Separate pipelined stages: A transfer->MWPM; B predecode->transfer->residual MWPM.
Fixed service times come from per-shot batch averages of the M3 CPU timings.
This is an optimistic amortized service model, not per-shot CPU measurements or
validated RTL timing. Transfer assumes sparse M2 framing on both paths, at
100 Mbit/s or 1 Gbit/s. Round arrivals: hypothetical 1/10/100 us.
1000 windows, rounds=3, deadline=1 ms from window start, unbounded FIFO, no drops.
Inflight occupancy includes queued and in-service windows, not a physical FIFO depth.

At 1 us/round: A has no deadline misses under the assumptions; B has 954 misses
at 100 Mbit/s and 953 at 1 Gbit/s, max prior inflight 875. At 10/100 us per round,
neither modeled architecture misses the specified deadline. These are model
outcomes, not measured real-time hardware behavior. A single fixed CPU average
cannot characterize tails; stochastic service and bounded capacity are future work.
